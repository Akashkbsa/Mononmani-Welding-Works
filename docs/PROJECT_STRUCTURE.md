# Mononmani Welding Works — project guide

Windows desktop app for GST tax invoices, customer records and payments. React + TypeScript + Tailwind v4 (Vite) for the window, Rust + Tauri 2 + SQLite for storage and logic. Everything runs offline on one computer.

Quotations, Purchase orders and Supplier bills also have the date filter; they open on **All dates** so nothing still open is hidden. The date filter (`components/MonthFilter.tsx`, rules in `src/lib/period.ts`) shows **All dates**, a **Month** or a **Financial year** (1 April to 31 March), each with ‹ › to step back and forward (never past this month or year), or a **Custom range** of days. The toolbars wrap onto a second line in a narrow window.

**Keyboard:** on every list screen `/` jumps to the search box and **Ctrl+N** starts a new record (invoice, quotation, purchase order, payment, supplier bill, supplier payment, company, reminder, tenant, place or employee). The Dashboard's Ctrl+N starts a new invoice. Both keys are ignored while typing or while a window is open (`src/lib/usePageShortcuts.ts`).

## Business rules (enforced in Rust)

- **Numbers:** `MWW/26-27/001` for invoices, `PAY/26-27/001` for payments: the Indian financial year (April–March, local time), then the running number. This is the final format. Numbers issued for a while in the swapped `MWW/001/26-27` form keep their number and still count towards the running number.
- **Vehicle number:** checked as an Indian registration (e.g. `TN 09 AB 1234`, `22 BH 1234 AA`) and stored in that spaced form; an older value that fails the check can stay until it is changed.
- **Purchase orders** hold the customer, PO number, PO date, status, an internal note and attached files; the app never reads the files. Files are kept inside the database (table `purchase_order_files`), up to 10 MB each, types JPG/PNG/PDF/DOC/DOCX, checked in Rust by size, extension and the file's first bytes. Status is set only by the owner. A duplicate PO number for the same customer is a warning, not an error.
- **Quotations** hold the customer, a quotation number typed by the owner, a quotation date (required), an internal note and attached files; no status, amounts or items, and the app never reads the files. Files follow the purchase order rules (inside the database, table `quotation_files`, 10 MB, same types and checks). A duplicate quotation number is a warning, not an error, and is checked across **all customers** because the numbers are the owner's own series.
- **Quotation <-> purchase order:** `purchase_orders.quotation_id` (nullable, migration 13). One quotation can have many purchase orders; an order has at most one quotation, and it must be the same customer's. Linking changes nothing printed. An order that keeps its quotation cannot change customer (remove the quotation first, even in the same save), and a quotation with linked orders cannot change customer or be deleted. From the quotation screen only that customer's orders with no quotation yet can be attached; an order tied to another quotation is refused so it never moves silently. A customer with quotations cannot be deleted.
- **Company type:** every company is a Customer, a Supplier or Both (`customers.party_type`, migration 14; existing companies are Customers). Invoices, payments, purchase orders and quotations accept only Customer or Both; supplier bills and supplier payments accept only Supplier or Both (`CustomerRepository::require_party`, called from each save). The type can't be changed to drop a side while records of that side exist (a Both company with invoices and supplier bills can go neither way), and a company with supplier bills or payments can't be deleted.
- **Supplier bills** hold the supplier, the supplier's own bill number (typed; a repeat for the same supplier is a warning only), date, one total amount, an internal note and files (same rules as quotation files). The status is worked out from the payments allocated to the bill, never stored. The total can't go below what is already settled, the supplier can't change once a payment is allocated, and a bill with payments can't be deleted.
- **Supplier payments** (`SP/26-27/001`) mirror customer payments without TDS: one payment can be applied to several of one supplier's bills. **There are no supplier advances** (removed in migration 19, which turned any old advance into a round-off and dropped the advance tables). Paying less can be accepted as full payment (a write-off, never more than what is actually short); paying more is always a round-off (counted as paid, nothing owed back). **There is no limit on a write-off or a round-off.** A payment must be applied to at least one bill. Editing recalculates as if the payment had never been recorded; deleting reopens its bills.
- **Rentals** (migration 16; tables `rental_places`, `rental_tenants`, `rent_months`, `rental_files`) are separate from invoices and companies. A place has at most one **current** tenant (no moved-out date; a partial unique index plus a message), so a new tenant needs the old one marked as moved out first. A tenant is on a month's list from the month they moved in (the month they were added when there is no move-in date) until the month they moved out; a month already marked paid always stays listed. **Paid** means the month's row has a paid date; a month with no row is Not paid. The recorded amount of a paid month never changes when the tenant's rent is edited later. Undoing a payment clears the date, amount and note and keeps the reminder date; a month left with nothing is removed. A place that has had tenants and a tenant with any rent record cannot be deleted (mark the tenant as moved out instead); files go with their place or tenant. Files follow the shared attachment rules (`rental_files`, `owner_kind` PLACE or TENANT, 10 MB, same types and checks).
- **Rent reminders:** the text is fixed: `Hello {name}, this is a reminder for your {Month Year} rent of {amount} for {place}. Please pay at your earliest convenience. Thank you.` (`₹12,000`, but `₹12,000.50`). Numbers are stored as `+` and digits: ten digits starting 6 to 9 become `+91…`, a typed `+` number keeps its country code. The app only opens `https://wa.me/<number>?text=<message>` (percent-encoded UTF-8) in the default program; the owner presses Send in WhatsApp, and nothing is ever sent automatically. Opening the chat with the message ready counts as sending the reminder (WhatsApp never says whether Send was pressed, and the owner chose not to confirm each one): each open is stored in `rent_reminders` (migration 17; migration 18 made earlier unconfirmed ones count as sent) and numbered per tenant and month (first, second follow-up, ...). A history is shown, and asking for another on the day one was sent needs a second press. A tenant who has paid never gets a reminder. Reminders are not rent records: they go with the tenant. Rental data has no PIN or encryption; it is in the same database, so every rental change is tracked in `change_log` and the Google Drive backup includes it.
- **WhatsApp numbers and supplier payment messages** (migration 21): a company's Phone is used for WhatsApp unless the form's "This phone number is also on WhatsApp" box is unticked and a separate number is typed (`customers.whatsapp_number`, optional, stored as `+` and digits like tenant numbers). A supplier payment's details window shows a message listing each bill it covers (`Invoice No & Date : {bill no} / {DD-MM-YYYY}`, `Amount : {bill's full total}`), then `Total amount` (sum of those bill totals) and `Amount sent` (the amount paid). **Send on WhatsApp** opens only a `https://wa.me/` link with it typed in; the owner presses Send. Opening it counts as sent and stores the day in `supplier_payments.whatsapp_sent_on` (shown as "Message sent" in the window and the list). With no WhatsApp number the phone is used; a landline or no number gives a clear error.
- **Reminders** (migration 20, table `reminders`) live only inside the app: no Windows notifications, no calendar sync. A reminder has a title, a date (`due_date`), `remind_days` (0–365), `repeat` (NONE/WEEKLY/MONTHLY/YEARLY, monthly with `month_end` for the last day of the month; `repeat_day` keeps the day it was set for so the 31st comes back after a short month and 29 February falls on 28 February) and an optional link (`link_kind` INVOICE, QUOTATION, PURCHASE_ORDER, PAYMENT, SUPPLIER_BILL, SUPPLIER_PAYMENT, COMPANY, TENANT or PLACE + `link_id`). On day T for date D: waiting before D − remind_days, then coming up (amber), due today (orange), overdue (red). The banner shows coming up / due / overdue ones that are not dismissed, overdue first, 3 then "and N more". **Snooze** (migration 23, `reminders.snoozed_until`) keeps it off the banner until a later day and it comes back by itself; it stays on the Reminders page as "Snoozed until …" with **Unsnooze**. The lengths offered are set in Settings (Tomorrow only by default; up to 5 of Tomorrow, 2 days, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, stored in `app_settings` as `reminder_snooze_days`); with one choice Snooze works in one click. Done and editing a reminder clear its snooze. **Done** finishes a one-time reminder; a repeating one moves one step to its next date (so a missed month is not skipped) and shows again. **Dismiss** only takes it off the banner. A deleted record leaves its reminder with "link removed". Every record's details window (and the tenant and place forms) has **Remind me**, which starts a reminder linked to it. Rules live in `reminders/repository.rs`; date rules in `reminders/schedule.rs`.
- **Employees and payroll** (migration 22; tables `employees`, `pay_months`, `employee_payments`, `employee_files`). An employee has a daily wage (more than 0) and an OT rate per hour (0 means no OT pay). Each month the owner enters days worked (0 up to the days employed that month, in half days; a joining or leaving date inside the month limits it) and OT hours (up to two decimals, at most 24 × days in the month). **Earned** = days × wage + OT hours × OT rate + an optional bonus, each line rounded to the paisa. The wage and OT rate are copied onto the month when it is first saved, so a later raise never changes it (it can be changed on that month; a raise in the middle of a month isn't split). Money for a month is an entry: **Advance** or **Salary** (money handed over, with a method, Cash by default), **Cash returned**, **Write off**, or **Take from next month**. **Balance** = earned − advances and salary + returned, written off and taken from next month − round-off; nothing is stored, it is all worked out (`payroll/calc.rs`). Each month stands alone: nothing carries over by itself. A salary with **Round off and finish** stores the difference (`round_off`, + paid less, − paid more) so the month is Paid; there is no size limit (the form warns above ₹100). Paying more without rounding off shows Overpaid. An overpaid month (it needs its days entered) is finished by **Take from next month** (one row: cleared on this month, an advance on the next; deleting it undoes both; not past the leaving month, at most one month after this one), **Cash returned** or **Write off**, each no more than what is overpaid. A month is open for entries when it is not after this month and is within the employee's joining and leaving months or already has a record; a later month holding a carried advance is shown but read-only until it starts. Editing a paid month reopens it and shows the difference. A month with payments can't be deleted; an employee with any record can't be deleted (mark as left). The leaving date can't be before joining or before the last month with records. **Rejoin** sets a new joining date after the leaving date and clears it; earlier months with records stay. The same name twice is a warning only. No loans, allowances, PF/ESI or pay slips. Rules live in `payroll/repository.rs`.
- **Invoice <-> purchase order:** `invoices.purchase_order_id` (nullable). One order can have many invoices; an invoice has at most one order, and it must be the same customer's. Linking fills the invoice's "PO number & date" text (`number & dd-mm-yyyy`, or just the number when the order has no date), so the printed invoice is unchanged. Unlinking, or later editing the order, leaves that text alone. An invoice that stays linked cannot change customer; removing the order first (even in the same save) allows it. An order with linked invoices cannot be deleted, and a customer with orders cannot be deleted. Cancelled and draft invoices can be linked.
- **Item descriptions** (invoice lines) can have several lines and a little formatting: select text in the box for a small bar with smaller, larger, bold and italic. They are stored in `invoice_items.description` as text with only `<b>`, `<i>`, `<font size="1-7">` and `<br>`; `util::sanitize_rich_text` (mirrored by `src/lib/richText.ts`) enforces that on save and again when printing, and shows any other `<` as plain text. The PDF template styles those tags.
- **PO / DC numbers** are entered as a number and a date and stored as `number & dd-mm-yyyy`; left empty they print as a dash.
- **TDS** can be entered as a percentage (0.1, 1, 2, 5, 10 or another rate) taken on the amount before GST or on the invoice total, worked out per invoice from the part each invoice is paid; or as a rupee amount. The payment still stores one `tds_amount`.
- **Printing** an invoice: any one copy, all three copies in one PDF, or original + duplicate in one PDF (`generate_invoice_copies_pdf`).
- **Customers** are picked with a type-to-search box (name, city, phone, GSTIN). An invoice shows its customer's details read-only; they are fixed when the customer is created or edited.
- **Tax state** comes from the GSTIN prefix when there is one; the forms warn when it disagrees with the state chosen.
- **Dates** are stored as `YYYY-MM-DD` and shown as `DD-MM-YYYY`.
- **Tax:** CGST + SGST for customers in Puducherry (state code 34), IGST otherwise. The state code comes from the GSTIN or the customer's state. Totals are exact to the paisa, with no round-off to the rupee, and the amount in words includes paise. Migration 11 brought older invoices that were not fully paid to their exact total; fully paid ones keep the total they were settled at, so they do not reopen for a few paise.
- **Invoice edits:** an invoice with payments cannot drop below what has been paid, cannot become Draft or Cancelled, and cannot be deleted.
- **Customers** with invoices or payments cannot be deleted.
- **Payment allocation:** a payment can only be applied to the same customer's Issued invoices, and never more than each invoice's balance or the money available.
- **Leftover money** is kept as an **advance**, marked **refunded**, or **kept as a round-off** (counted as received, nothing owed back, no advance). There is no size limit; the owner chooses.
- **Short payments:** the owner can accept a payment that is a little short as full payment ("Settle in full"). The shortfall is stored on the payment as `writeoff_amount` (migration 10), counts towards settling the invoices like recognised TDS, and has no size limit (the form warns above ₹100). It must equal what is actually short after money received, TDS and any advance used. Deleting the payment reopens the invoices. Advances are used oldest-first by later payments ("Use advance" in the payment form). Deleting or editing a payment gives back any advance it used. A payment whose advance has already been used cannot be deleted until the later payment is removed.

## Data on disk

`%APPDATA%\com.manonmani.app\` — `Database\manonmani.db` (also holds purchase order, quotation, supplier bill and rental files), `Invoices\<financial year>\<number>_<copy>.pdf` (e.g. `Invoices\2026-27`; PDFs saved before this change stay in their calendar-year folder), `Documents\<sha256>` (Document Vault files, each named by its SHA-256 and checked against it when read), `Temp\` (short-lived copies of attached files made so they can be opened, named `po_<id>_<name>`, `quotation_<id>_<name>`, `supplier_bill_<id>_<name>` or `rental_<id>_<name>` or `employee_<id>_<name>` or `vault_<id>_<name>` so the kinds never mix; emptied each time the app starts). The folder name comes from the Tauri identifier; don't change the identifier or the app will start with an empty database.

## Building and running

- `app.bat` — the only launcher: shows a menu, or use `app.bat dev` (`npm run tauri dev`), `app.bat build` (release build), `app.bat open` (start the built app).
- `npm run build` — type-check and build the UI only; `npm run typecheck` only type-checks.
- `npm run test:rust` — the Rust tests (same as `tools\test-harness\test.bat`).

PDF creation needs Microsoft Edge (installed with Windows) or Google Chrome.

## Tests

UI unit tests are `*.test.ts(x)` files next to the code they test, run by Vitest in jsdom (`vitest.config.ts`, setup in `src/test/setup.ts`). They cover the `lib/` helpers, the shared kit in `components/ui.tsx`, the filters, pickers, banners, the attachments hook and every form dialog (invoice, payment, supplier payment, purchase order, quotation, supplier bill, customer, employee, wage entry, pay month, place, tenant, mark paid, rent reminders, reminder, business settings), with `services/api` or Tauri mocked. Shared test helpers are in `src/test/helpers.ts` (`field` finds a control by its `Field` label, `pick` chooses from a searchable list, `makeCustomer`, `makeFile`). Invoice totals are worked out in `src/lib/invoiceTotals.ts` and in `calculate_financials` in Rust; both are checked against the same cases in `src/test/fixtures/invoice_totals.json`, so what the form shows is what gets saved. Add a case there when the arithmetic changes. `services/api.test.ts` also reads the Rust sources and checks that every command the UI calls is registered in `lib.rs` and is sent exactly the arguments its Rust function takes.

Rust unit tests live next to the code (`customers/repository.rs`, `invoices/repository.rs`, `payments/repository.rs`, `purchase_orders/repository.rs`, `quotations/repository.rs`, `supplier_bills/repository.rs`, `supplier_payments/repository.rs`, `rentals/repository.rs`, `rentals/reminder.rs`, `reminders/schedule.rs`, `reminders/repository.rs`, `payroll/calc.rs`, `payroll/repository.rs`, `attachments.rs`, `db/migrations.rs`, `util.rs`, `pdf/renderer.rs`). They cover tax maths, number-to-words, migrations, allocation limits, advances, and the delete/edit guards.

On this machine plain `cargo test` builds but the test executable cannot start (`STATUS_ENTRYPOINT_NOT_FOUND`): the MinGW linker can't merge Tauri's Windows manifest into test binaries. Run the Rust tests with `tools\test-harness\test.bat` instead: it compiles the data-layer modules (`db`, `util`, `attachments`, and the `models.rs`/`repository.rs` files of customers, invoices, payments, purchase_orders, quotations, supplier_bills, supplier_payments, rentals, reminders, payroll, settings and backup, plus `payroll/calc.rs`, `rentals/reminder.rs`, `documents/files.rs` and `sync.rs`, `backup/snapshot.rs` and `pdf/renderer.rs`) in a small crate without Tauri, using `#[path]` modules. Add a new module there when you add one to the app.

Integration tests are in `tools/test-harness/tests/integration.rs`. They make records through one module and check them from another on a real migrated database: invoices with payments and advances, the Customer/Supplier/Both rules, quotation → purchase order → invoice links, supplier bills and payments, reminders linked to every kind of record, attachments removed with their record, every record table watched by the backup change log, a snapshot restored intact, and an invoice from the database filling every field of the PDF template.

## Google Drive backup

- `src-tauri/src/backup/`: `snapshot.rs` (VACUUM INTO copy, integrity check, gzip, hashes), `repository.rs` (change log summary, run history, last known Drive space), `drive.rs` (OAuth with PKCE, Credential Manager storage, Drive calls), `schedule.rs` (when the daily backup is due, which old backups are thinned, how full Drive is; no network or SQL), `commands.rs` (including the automatic backup thread).
- Migration 8 adds `change_log` (filled by triggers on every record table) and `backup_runs`. A backup is skipped when nothing changed after the last successful run; the change number only advances after the upload is verified (size and MD5), so interrupted backups lose nothing. Migration 24 adds the owner's settings (business, bank, tax rates, snooze choices) and supplier payment allocations to the log, so changing only the settings still starts the daily backup; the backup's own `drive_space` note and saving a value unchanged are not counted.
- Every backup is a complete compressed copy and is never overwritten, so earlier versions stay restorable. Restore keeps a `before-restore-*.db` copy in the Backups folder.
- **Automatic daily backup:** while the app is open, a background thread checks one minute after start and then every 15 minutes. It backs up when Drive is connected, nothing was backed up today and something changed. Offline is not recorded as a failure (it simply tries again), and the same failure is recorded only once a day. The window gets a `backup-auto` event after an automatic backup worked or failed, and shows a short message. **Back up now** still works at any time.
- **Keeping backups:** after each successful upload, backups older than two years (by the date in the file name) keep only the newest of each month; the others are deleted. Backups from the last two years are all kept. Only the app's own `manonmani-YYYY-MM-DD_HHMMSS-seqN.db.gz` files in the Mononmani Backups folder are ever touched.
- **Document Vault:** each backup first uploads the vault documents that have no copy yet, once each, into a separate **Mononmani Documents** folder (file name as added, `appProperties` sha256 and group), then makes the database copy, which so knows where they are. A copy is checked (size and MD5) before it counts; one already on Drive (found by its sha256) is only noted. Documents waiting also make the daily backup due. Nothing in that folder is ever deleted or thinned: the store in `documents/sync.rs` has no delete, and thinning only reads the Mononmani Backups folder. After a restore, documents the restored data lists but this computer lacks are downloaded and checked against their SHA-256; one never backed up is named in the message. Opening a missing document fetches it the same way.
- **Drive space:** before uploading, the app asks Google how much room is left. If the backup does not fit (or Google refuses it as full) it stops and remembers FULL; under 500 MB free is LOW (`app_settings` key `drive_space`). LOW shows an amber banner and FULL a red one at the top of every screen, telling the owner to open Google Drive and delete the oldest backups in the Mononmani Backups folder (**Open Google Drive**, and **Check again** when full). The app never deletes recent backups to make room.
- **Settings dot:** the sidebar's Settings shows a dot when Drive is not connected, Drive is almost full or full, or there are changes not backed up since before today.
- The Google Client ID/secret and refresh token live in Windows Credential Manager, not in the database.
- The data-layer tests run through `tools/test-harness` (see Tests above).
